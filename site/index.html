    identity/docs

    From ticker to a live coin in five steps.

    The idea is simple. The details matter, so here is every one of them.

    1. 01

      Choose your ticker

      Type the ticker you want. We check it against the base58 alphabet and show how long its address takes, before you commit to anything.

      valid a–k m–z 1–9 invalid l 0 spaces symbols case case-insensitive
    2. 02

      We find your address

      GPU workers generate random keypairs until one has an address that starts with your ticker. Short tickers are already waiting in our pool.

      loop: kp = new ed25519 keypair if base58(kp.pubkey).startsWith("fire") → match
    3. 03

      Sign once

      You approve one transaction from your wallet. Our server adds the mint key's signature, then deletes the key. It is never used again.

      signers your wallet + mint key mint key lives for one transaction
    4. 04

      Live on pump.fun

      Your coin is created on pump.fun's bonding curve like any other and trades there from the first second.

      program pump.fun curve pump.fun rules migration pump.fun rules
    5. 05

      Check it yourself

      Paste the address into any explorer. The ticker is built into the address, so it can't be edited or faked after launch.

      solscan.io/token/fire… pump.fun/coin/fire…
    identity/math

    Why short tickers are instant and long ones wait.

    A Solana address is the public half of a random keypair, written in base58. Nobody can pick one directly, so we generate keypairs until one starts with your ticker. That search is called grinding.

    Each address character has 58 possible values. Every letter you add makes the search about 58 times longer. Four letters take a blink. Eight letters take more than a day of GPU time.

    Base58 leaves out characters that look alike: 0 O I l. A ticker with a lowercase L or a zero can never be matched.

    identity/trust

    Check what you can. Know what you can't.

    Some of Identity is verifiable on chain. Some of it relies on us. Here is exactly which is which.

    On chain

    Verify it yourself on any Solana explorer.

    • The contract address, and that it starts with your ticker.
    • The launch: a standard pump.fun create transaction on pump.fun's own bonding curve.
    • The creator wallet and any first buy it made, in the same public transaction.

    Off chain

    Handled by our servers. You are trusting us here.

    • Grinding runs on our GPU workers.
    • The mint key is generated on our server, signs your create transaction once, then is deleted.
    • The pre-ground pool is tracked so no address is ever handed out twice.

    The risks

    A matching address proves where a coin came from. It says nothing about whether the coin is worth anything. Identity launches on pump.fun, so pump.fun's rules, fees and migration apply. Memecoins are high risk; only spend what you can afford to lose.

    identity/faq

    The questions people ask first.

    Is it really free?+

    Yes. Identity takes no platform fee. You pay the Solana network fees and pump.fun's own fees, exactly as you would launching on pump.fun.

    Is this a real pump.fun coin?+

    Yes. Identity sends a standard pump.fun create transaction. The only difference is the mint address, which we choose so it starts with your ticker.

    Why can't my ticker contain l or 0?+

    Solana addresses use base58, which leaves out 0, O, I and l because they look alike. An address can never contain those characters, so no amount of grinding can match them.

    Is the prefix case-sensitive?+

    No. Matching is case-insensitive, so $FIRE might land an address like FiRe… or fIrE…. Base58 uses mixed-case letters, so the exact casing varies with each grind.

    How long does a launch take?+

    Two to four letters: instant. Five to six: seconds to about a minute. Seven to eight: queued and delivered later. The checker shows the estimate for your exact ticker.

    Can two coins share a ticker?+

    Yes. Each launch gets its own freshly ground address, so two $fire coins both start with fire but are otherwise completely different. No address is ever reused.

    What happens to the mint key?+

    It signs your create transaction once on our server and is then deleted. We never store it and never use it again.

    What about the vanity wallet generator?+

    The wallet generator lets you create a personal Solana wallet whose address starts with any prefix you choose. It requires holding 100,000 Identity tokens to prevent spam and keep GPU resources available. Your private key is encrypted during grinding and deleted from our servers the instant you reveal it — or after 10 minutes if you never do. It exists in your browser and nowhere else.

      identity/under-the-hood

      What happens when you press launch.

      About ten seconds for most tickers. Your wallet pays and signs; Identity never holds your funds.

      01

      Upload

      Your image and details go to IPFS, the same storage pump.fun uses. The banner stays on Identity.

      POST /api/metadata → ipfs://…
      02

      Grind

      If your ticker is in the pool, the address is reserved for your wallet instantly. Otherwise a GPU starts searching and you see your place in the queue.

      POST /api/grind → fire…
      03

      Sign

      PumpPortal builds the create transaction with your wallet as the payer. Your wallet signs it first, then Identity adds the address's signature.

      wallet.signTransaction(tx)
      04

      The coin is created on pump.fun. Its address key is deleted from our server, and the coin appears in the feed and the coins grid.
